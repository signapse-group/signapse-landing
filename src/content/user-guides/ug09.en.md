## Terms

This page explains terms used in the guides. When reading a state, check the area name, scope, and accompanying message; the same word may describe different objects.

### Account and tracking space

| Term        | Meaning for users                                                                                                                                                                                | Read more                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Account     | The identity you use to access Signapse. Signing in successfully does not grant access to every feature.                                                                                         | [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02) |
| Permission  | A condition allowing you to view data or perform an action. Viewing and editing permissions may differ.                                                                                          | [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02) |
| Usage limit | A usage restriction for a particular metric and scope, such as assets per workspace or AI turns.                                                                                                 | [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02) |
| Workspace   | Your personal space for organizing the tracking experience. The active workspace is the one currently selected for use.                                                                          | [Workspaces and watchlists](/en/docs/ug03)       |
| Watchlist   | The assets you select for a workspace, defining its asset research scope. Adding or removing assets does not create or delete private copies of market knowledge.                                | [Workspaces and watchlists](/en/docs/ug03)       |
| Scope       | The information being viewed or analyzed according to the workspace, assets, time, search terms, and filters applied by the screen. Read each area's description to understand its actual scope. | [Reading the Overview](/en/docs/ug04)            |

### Market information and AI

| Term                              | Meaning for users                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Read more                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Asset                             | A market entity tracked by Signapse. Check its name, symbol, and type to select the intended asset.                                                                                                                                | [Workspaces and watchlists](/en/docs/ug03)         |
| Event asset roles                 | The primary subject is the event's central subject; an affected asset is one interpreted as experiencing an impact; a reference asset supplies context. Being mentioned does not establish a price impact.                         | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| News article                      | A specific article brought into Signapse. Available content may be partial or only a short description.                                                                                                                            | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| News source / Source article      | A news outlet publishes information; a source article is the specific article referenced. A source name does not replace that article's content and link.                                                                          | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| Event                             | Market-relevant information normalized from input data. Its description needs checking against evidence.                                                                                                                           | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| Theme                             | A topic connecting events and interpretations, such as inflation or market sentiment.                                                                                                                                              | [Reading the Overview](/en/docs/ug04)              |
| Market reaction                   | A price or market response observed or inferred in relation to an event. Distinguish observations from impact interpretations.                                                                                                     | [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06)             |
| Narrative                         | A broader interpretation connecting events and reactions over time. It is not a certain conclusion.                                                                                                                                | [Reading the Overview](/en/docs/ug04)              |
| Evidence                          | Information or articles used to support an event or interpretation. Roles may include Primary, Supporting, Update, or Contradicting.                                                                                               | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| Provenance                        | The ability to trace an interpretation or event back to the sources that contributed to it and check their content.                                                                                                                | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| Confidence                        | The system's stated confidence in the content being considered. It does not replace source checking or represent the probability of a price rise or fall.                                                                          | [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) |
| Candle / Timeframe                | A candle aggregates open, high, low, and close data over a timeframe. For example, 1h means a one-hour candle, distinct from the total history range being viewed.                                                                 | [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06)             |
| Chart annotation / Context marker | Content placed at a timestamp for comparison with prices. For an event, the time may come from the impact interpretation's observation time; it is not necessarily the event occurrence time or a measured price reaction.         | [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06)             |
| HOT_EVENT / WARM_EPISODE          | Two chart context types: an event / a summary of an asset's context for a completed month. Do not read the monthly summary as a single event at the marker's position.                                                             | [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06)             |
| Time zone / UTC                   | A time zone is a way of expressing an instant. UTC is a shared time reference; check displayed time zones when comparing events, prices, and usage reset times.                                                                    | [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06)             |
| Conversation / AI turn            | A conversation is a saved sequence of questions and responses in a workspace context. AI turns shows usage returned by the app; resubmitting or opening history does not update old answers automatically.                         | [Working with the AI Assistant](/en/docs/ug07)     |
| AI memory / Conversation history  | Recent memory helps AI understand follow-ups; history consists of saved messages you can read again. Being able to read older messages does not mean AI remembers the entire history; new turns still apply the current watchlist. | [Working with the AI Assistant](/en/docs/ug07)     |
| Data limitation                   | Data or support missing, insufficient, or unverified for the current question or scope. Different from an account usage limit.                                                                                                     | [Working with the AI Assistant](/en/docs/ug07)     |

### Telegram

| Term                      | Meaning for users                                                                                                                                           | Read more                                        |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Telegram bot / Bot token  | A bot is an automated account used for notifications; its token is the secret used to connect it. Do not include tokens in support requests or screenshots. | [Setting up Telegram](/en/docs/ug10)             |
| Destination               | A Telegram conversation linked to receive notifications through a bot, such as a private chat or group.                                                     | [Setting up Telegram](/en/docs/ug10)             |
| Destination linking       | Verifying a receiving conversation through the bot. Creating a linking command does not mean linking is complete.                                           | [Setting up Telegram](/en/docs/ug10)             |
| Test message              | A message sent to a destination using Send test. Receiving it does not confirm that a market alert flow is enabled or has produced a notification.          | [Setting up Telegram](/en/docs/ug10)             |
| Routing                   | Settings selecting the destination and enabled state for a notification flow. Having a bot does not mean a destination has been selected for each flow.     | [Setting up Telegram](/en/docs/ug10)             |
| Default Telegram language | The language used when notifications are set to Default; selected separately from the interface language.                                                   | [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02) |

## Data states

### Reading data or using features

| Common sign                                               | Meaning                                                                                                                                                   | Action / Related guide                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Loading / Processing / Thinking                           | The request is being processed; it is not a completed result.                                                                                             | Wait for completion. For AI, see [UG07](/en/docs/ug07).                       |
| Zero                                                      | A count of zero in the returned scope; different from usage data failing to load.                                                                         | Read the metric, time, and scope using [UG04](/en/docs/ug04).                 |
| No data / No results / No evidence yet                    | The corresponding information is absent from the current result. It does not establish that there are no market events or that a claim has been verified. | Check scope, search terms, and the missing part using [UG08](/en/docs/ug08).  |
| No permission / Unavailable with a permission description | The account lacks permission for that area or action. It does not mean the data is zero.                                                                  | See [UG02](/en/docs/ug02); contact the person who granted access when needed. |
| Could not load / Error                                    | Reading or processing did not finish successfully; do not treat the blank area as a valid empty result.                                                   | Read the message and retry when available; see [UG08](/en/docs/ug08).         |
| Stale / Live data is stale / Reconnecting                 | Visible information may not reflect the latest updates.                                                                                                   | Check update times and status using [UG06](/en/docs/ug06).                    |
| Market closed                                             | A market state reported for the price feed; it does not by itself mean the chart failed.                                                                  | Read it alongside the update time using [UG06](/en/docs/ug06).                |
| Over limit                                                | A metric or selection exceeds the applicable limit. Different from missing evidence or a loading error.                                                   | Compare the message and usage using [UG02](/en/docs/ug02).                    |

### Editing and saving

| Common sign                  | Meaning                                                                                                                                            | Action / Related guide                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unsaved / Saving             | Edits have not been confirmed as saved successfully.                                                                                               | Keep your content and wait for the result using [UG03](/en/docs/ug03).                        |
| Updated / Saved successfully | Saving was reported successful; check that the result applies to the intended object and scope. It does not confirm that dependent flows have run. | Reopen the relevant list or information; see [UG03](/en/docs/ug03) and [UG10](/en/docs/ug10). |
| No changes to save           | The selection matches the loaded state; it is not a new data update.                                                                               | Check the list if it differs from what you expected using [UG03](/en/docs/ug03).              |
| Save failed / Partly saved   | Not all changes are confirmed saved. In particular, some watchlist changes may have succeeded before an error.                                     | Compare the current state before resubmitting using [UG03](/en/docs/ug03).                    |

### Setting up Telegram

| Common sign                                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                          | Action / Related guide                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Needs setup / Needs linking / Needs review | A bot, destination, or routing setting needs checking. Read each area's description, including any permission restriction.                                       | Check within your permissions using [UG10](/en/docs/ug10).                          |
| Ready / Routes enabled                     | A configuration area meets readiness conditions or a flow is enabled according to current data. It does not establish that a specific notification was received. | Distinguish saved settings from received notifications using [UG10](/en/docs/ug10). |
| Test send successful                       | Signapse reports a successful test send; check for the new message in the correct Telegram conversation.                                                         | Compare the bot and destination using [UG10](/en/docs/ug10).                        |
| Test send timed out                        | The send result is uncertain; the message may have reached Telegram.                                                                                             | Check the conversation before resending using [UG10](/en/docs/ug10).                |

Event processing states, narrative states, and evidence roles describe different aspects; a label such as Active or Completed does not by itself establish content accuracy. If a state is unclear or a problem persists, see [Missing data or features](/en/docs/ug08) and contact the person who granted access for support.
