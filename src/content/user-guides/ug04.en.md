## Goal

Identify the workspace in use, read the information areas on the Overview, and choose an event or article to check further. Distinguish time, data scope, permission restrictions, empty states, and errors before interpreting the information.

## Prerequisites and permissions

You have been granted access to Signapse, have workspace reading permission, and have selected an active workspace. Asset, watchlist, event, news, and narrative reading permissions determine which data areas you can view. See [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02).

To follow assets you care about, check the workspace watchlist first. Do not assume every asset already has news, events, or evidence.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Overview and check the workspace

Select **Overview** in the app navigation. Compare the workspace name on the page with the workspace selector at the top of the app.

Under **Tracked assets**, read the names, symbols, and types in the selected workspace's full watchlist. Compare the count with the list; do not treat it as a shortened list. To check or change the list when permitted, select **Manage tracked assets**. See [Workspaces and watchlists](/en/docs/ug03).

### 2. Read the information areas

| Area                  | How to read it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Summary cards         | Read **Next key event**, **Market events**, **Market narratives**, and **Latest news** together with the description under each value. Metrics have their own time windows or status conditions; do not compare them as if they represented the same dataset.                                                 |
| **Event timeline**    | Read the title, description, themes, affected assets, occurrence time, and confidence when available. These are recent events in the current tracking scope.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Latest news**       | Read the title, short description, news source, and publication time. This area shows recent articles across Signapse; do not assume every article relates to your watchlist.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Assets in focus**   | Read the asset name, symbol, type, accompanying context, and observation time. A prominent position is a starting point for investigation, not a buy or sell instruction. If permitted and **Open Market Charts** is available, open it for further exploration using [Reading market charts](/en/docs/ug06). |
| **Market narratives** | Read the title, interpretation, status, theme, related assets, confidence, and update time when available. A narrative is a market interpretation whose supporting evidence needs checking, not a certain conclusion.                                                                                         |

### 3. Distinguish times and data states

Read the time attached to each item: the workspace time is its last modification, events have occurrence times, articles have publication times, asset context has an observation time, and narratives have an update time. Do not use one timestamp to conclude that the entire page was updated at once.

For count cards, read the displayed scope description, such as events in the last 24 hours or articles published in the last 6 hours. Event timeline shows up to five of the latest events for the watchlist and may include events older than 24 hours; Latest news shows up to five of the latest articles across Signapse. The card counts therefore need not equal the item counts in these two lists. Tracked assets still shows the full watchlist.

Wait for loading to finish. Distinguish **0** from **No data**, **Unavailable** accompanied by a permission description, and **Could not load**. Read each area's own message; one area's error or empty state does not establish the same state for the rest of the page.

### 4. Open an event or article to check it

In **Event timeline**, select a title to open its detail panel. Read the description, occurrence time, related assets, and **Main evidence** when available. From an evidence item, use **View article** when permitted or **Open original** when a link is available to compare the external source.

In **Latest news**, select a title to open the article detail panel. Check the source, publication time, and content; use the original link when available to check the source article.

When needed, use the full-page action in the detail panel, or close the panel to return to the Overview. **View all events** and **View all news**, when displayed, lead to their respective lists; check the scope and filters on the destination page. See [Reading news, events and evidence](/en/docs/ug05) for detailed source checking.

## Expected result

You know which workspace you are viewing, can distinguish each area's scope, time, and data state, and can open an event or article to check supporting sources within your permissions. You can identify missing information rather than treating the summary as the complete market context.

## Troubleshooting

- **No workspace or the wrong tracked asset list:** select the intended workspace and check its watchlist using [Workspaces and watchlists](/en/docs/ug03).
- **An area is hidden or reports missing permissions:** read its description and [Account, permissions and limits](/en/docs/ug02). Contact the person who granted access when your account needs checking.
- **Data is empty, a time is missing, or loading fails:** read the affected area's message and follow [Missing data or features](/en/docs/ug08). Do not interpret empty data as the absence of market events.
- **Details will not open or the source article is missing:** note what you could not check. Use **Retry** if available; if the problem persists, provide the item name, workspace, message, and time of occurrence to the person who granted access for support.
